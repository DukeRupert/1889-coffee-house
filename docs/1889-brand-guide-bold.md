# 1889 Coffee House
## Brand & UI Guide — Bold Direction

*Firefly Software · Client Pitch Document*

---

## Brand Foundation

**Business:** 1889 Coffee House
**Location:** 1800 Prospect Ave, Helena, MT 59601
**Ownership:** Family owned & operated — Brett Wiensch, Ashley Wiensch, Danielle Yatsko. Veteran owned.
**Positioning:** Helena's most distinctive coffee house. Named for Montana statehood. Built from the ground up — literally — on a demolished gas station on Prospect Ave.

**Brand truth:** *This isn't a chain and it doesn't look like one. The wall says 1889 in letters two feet tall. The website should feel the same way.*

---

## Design Direction

Derived from the physical interior — specifically the wall-mounted logo: bold condensed numerals, coffee cup silhouette, em-dash flanked wordmark, all in near-black on cranberry. The digital brand extends that energy rather than softening it.

**Register:** Vintage-athletic. Confident. Warm through boldness, not pastels.

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Cranberry | `#A64153` | Hero left panel, section accents, logo bg, CTA hover |
| Base | Near-black | `#111111` | Hero right panel, nav, footer, dark sections |
| Primary text | White | `#ffffff` | All text on dark and cranberry backgrounds |
| Muted text | Warm white | `rgba(255,255,255,0.65)` | Subheads and body on dark bg |
| Subtle text | Faded white | `rgba(255,255,255,0.45)` | Labels, nav links, secondary info on dark bg |
| Divider / accent | Cranberry muted | `rgba(166,65,83,0.25)` | Borders, dividers, ring accents on dark bg |
| Light surface | Off-white | `#f7f4f0` | Body sections on light bg (between dark panels) |
| Body text | Espresso | `#2d1910` | Body copy on light/off-white backgrounds |

**Usage rules:**
- Cranberry and near-black are the two load-bearing colors. Everything else serves them.
- Never use cranberry as a text color — it's always a background or accent element.
- The split panel pattern (cranberry left / near-black right) is the signature layout move. Use it for the hero. Can be inverted (near-black left / cranberry right) for variety in later sections.
- Light off-white sections exist to give the eye a rest between dark panels — use sparingly, one or two per page.
- White text only on cranberry or near-black backgrounds. Never white on off-white.

## Logo

The wall logo is the brand mark. Recreate faithfully for digital use:

**Structure:**
- Coffee cup silhouette with steam lines — centered above the numerals
- "1889" — massive, Barlow Condensed ExtraBold 800, tight letter-spacing
- Horizontal rule flanking the cup above; em-dash flanking "COFFEE HOUSE" below
- "COFFEE HOUSE" — Barlow Condensed Bold, wide letter-spacing (~0.28em), all-caps

**Digital colors:**
- On cranberry bg → White mark
- On near-black bg → White mark with cranberry rules/dashes
- On off-white bg → Near-black mark with cranberry rules/dashes
- Nav favicon → Cranberry square, white "1889" numerals

## Typography

All fonts from Google Fonts — no licensing required.

| Role | Font | Style | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headlines / logo | Barlow Condensed | ExtraBold 800 | Hero numerals, section headlines |
| Subheads / eyebrows | Barlow Condensed | Bold 700 / SemiBold 600 | Section labels, nav, CTAs |
| Body copy | Barlow | Regular 400 | All prose on light and dark backgrounds |
| UI labels | Barlow Condensed | SemiBold 600, all-caps, tracked | Buttons, hours, credentials |

**Scale:**
- Logo numerals: 100–120px, line-height 0.85
- Hero headline: 68–72px, line-height 0.92, uppercase
- Section headline: 42–48px, line-height 1.0
- Subheadline: 22–26px
- Body: 15–16px, line-height 1.7
- Eyebrow/label: 10–12px, letter-spacing 0.18–0.26em, uppercase

**Eyebrow system:**
Short em-dash rule + Barlow Condensed SemiBold all-caps label. Structural, not cursive — matches the logo's em-dash flanking pattern.

```
— Helena, Montana · Est. 1889    ← dash + Barlow Condensed, rgba(255,255,255,0.6)
Montana's Coffee House.          ← Barlow Condensed 800, white, uppercase
```

No Sacramento cursive. No Clarendon slab serif. The wall logo is the typographic authority — Barlow Condensed owns the brand.

## Layout Principles

**Hero:** Split panel. Cranberry left (headline + subhead + CTAs) / Near-black right (logo lockup + hours). Subtle diagonal stripe texture on cranberry panel. Concentric ring accent on dark panel.

**Section alternation:**
1. Near-black (hero)
2. Off-white (origin story — single column, breathing room)
3. Cranberry (feature callout — nitro cold brew, or food)
4. Near-black (Ghost Town coffee sourcing)
5. Off-white (gather / community)
6. Near-black (hours + contact footer)

**Cards on dark bg:** Cranberry border (`rgba(166,65,83,0.3)`), near-black fill, white text. No drop shadows — flat surfaces only.

**Buttons:**
- Primary: Near-black fill, white text, 2px border-radius. On cranberry bg only.
- Secondary: Transparent, white border (`rgba(255,255,255,0.4)`), white text. On cranberry or dark bg.
- On light bg: Cranberry fill, white text.

**Dividers:** 1px cranberry-muted (`rgba(166,65,83,0.25)`) on dark sections. 1px warm grey on light sections.

**Texture:** Repeating diagonal line pattern (`45deg`, 12px pitch, 4% black opacity) on cranberry panels adds tactile depth without photography. Use consistently on all cranberry backgrounds.

**Ring accent:** Two concentric thin-border circles on near-black panels behind the logo lockup. Cranberry at 15–20% opacity. Purely decorative — suggests the stamp/badge quality of the logo mark.
