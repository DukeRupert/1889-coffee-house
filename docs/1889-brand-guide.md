# 1889 Coffee House
## Brand & UI Guide

## Brand Foundation

**Business:** 1889 Coffee House
**Location:** 1800 Prospect Ave, Helena, MT 59601
**Ownership:** Family owned & operated — Brett Wiensch, Ashley Wiensch, Danielle Yatsko. Veteran owned.
**Positioning:** Helena's community gathering place. Local, warm, unhurried. Named for the day Montana became a state — November 8, 1889.

**Brand truth:** *Built from the ground up — literally. A demolished gas station became Helena's favorite morning ritual.*

---

## Voice & Tone

- Warm and unhurried. Never rushed, never corporate.
- Community-first. The name, the space, the food — everything connects back to Helena and Montana.
- Specific over generic. "Eggs cracked in-house" beats "fresh ingredients."
- Let the story carry the weight. The 1889 origin, the family, the Ghost Town partnership — these are real differentiators.
- Sacramento eyebrow words set the emotional register before the headline lands. One evocative word. A period. That's it.

**Avoid:**
- Chain coffee shop language ("handcrafted," "artisanal," "premium experience")
- Anything that could belong to a franchise
- Dark or moody copy — the brand is warm, not brooding

## Color Palette

All colors derived from existing Wix theme (`--color_*` variables).

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Text / deep bg | Espresso | `#2d1910` | Headlines, one accent section only |
| Subheads | Dark roast | `#512c1d` | Subheadings, body on light bg |
| Primary CTA | Medium roast | `#7d6055` | Buttons, links, eyebrows on white |
| Soft background | Cream | `#d3bbb2` | Dividers, card borders, subtle bg |
| Warm section bg | Pale gold | `#f9e3bb` | Secondary section backgrounds |
| Highlight | Honey | `#edaf3b` | Eyebrows on dark bg, highlights |
| Special accent | Montana sky | `#0088cb` | One use only — origin story eyebrow |
| Base | White | `#ffffff` | Dominant background throughout |

**Background hierarchy — most to least used:**
1. White `#ffffff` — dominant, most sections
2. Pale gold `#f9e3bb` — secondary section breaks
3. Cream `#d3bbb2` — subtle dividers and card accents
4. Espresso `#2d1910` — one section only, used like punctuation

The owners favor strong whitespace. Espresso as a background is a single deliberate moment, not a pattern.

**Montana sky blue rule:** Reserved for one eyebrow only — the Montana origin/statehood section. Never used elsewhere. The restraint is what gives it power.

### The Eyebrow System

Every major section opens with a one-word Sacramento cursive eyebrow followed by a Clarendon headline. The eyebrow sets the emotional tone; the headline delivers the message.

**Format:**
```
taste.                    ← Sacramento, ~28px, color varies by bg
More Than Just Great Coffee   ← Clarendon, large, espresso or white
```

**Eyebrow color by background:**
- On white bg → Medium roast `#7d6055`
- On pale gold bg → Dark roast `#512c1d`
- On espresso bg → Honey `#edaf3b`
- Origin/Montana section → Montana sky `#0088cb` (one use only)

**Suggested eyebrow words by section:**
- `taste.` — menu / coffee section
- `gather.` — community / about section
- `fresh.` — food section
- `rooted.` — Montana origin story section
- `pour.` — Ghost Town coffee sourcing section
- `mornings.` — hours / visit section

**Rules:**
- One word only. Always lowercase. Always followed by a period.
- Never use more than one eyebrow per section.
- Sacramento is an eyebrow font only — never used for body copy or headlines.

## Layout Principles

**Whitespace is the brand.** The owners clearly favor open, breathing layouts. Don't fill every section. Let the typography and imagery do the work.

**Image philosophy:** Stock coffee photos (beans on wood) are placeholders. The real assets are the Donnie Sexton photos from the Montana Standard feature — Brett, Ashley, and Danielle; the actual food; the real space. These should be sourced and used wherever possible. The loft with mountain views is a hero image waiting to happen.
